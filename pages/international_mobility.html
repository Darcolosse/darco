International Mobility Ideas

Welcome to the International Mobility Ideas section of my E-Portfolio. Here you will find various suggestions and opportunities for experiencing life abroad.

Why go for international mobility?

Going abroad for a few months or a semester is a unique and enriching experience. For me, international mobility is an opportunity to discover new horizons:

  • Cultural experience: Discovering new cultures, traditions, and ways of life broadens your worldview and helps you better understand cultural differences.
  • Language skills: Living abroad is an excellent opportunity to improve your skills in a foreign language through total immersion.
  • Personal development: Adapting to a new environment, stepping out of your comfort zone, and taking on new challenges fosters autonomy, self-confidence, and open-mindedness.
Slovakia
Why Slovakia?

Slovakia is a country with a rich historical and cultural heritage, offering an authentic experience to international students. Its central location in Europe makes it easy to explore neighboring countries. Slovakia attracts with its preserved natural landscapes, medieval castles, and varied gastronomy.

My professional experience

I had the opportunity to go to Slovakia for 3 months as part of a professional assignment. I work at Orange Wholesale, within the OINIS entity (Orange International Networks Infrastructures and Services), in the TCI team (Telco Cloud Infrastructure). Our team is responsible for designing and developing SDNPOPv2, a technology aimed at virtualizing network infrastructures. During my stay, I joined the Build & Run team of OINIS at Orange Business, where I discovered the operational aspect of SDNPOPv2: the deployment and maintenance of infrastructures.

The team based in Bratislava allowed me to immerse myself in Slovak culture, taste local cuisine, and explore the country's beautiful landscapes.

My personal experience

Bratislava enjoys a strategic position in the heart of Central Europe, close to iconic cities such as Vienna (Austria), Budapest (Hungary), Prague (Czech Republic), and Krakow (Poland). This location allowed me to travel easily and discover the cultural diversity of the region.

This experience in Slovakia was extremely enriching, both professionally and personally. I developed new skills, improved my English, and expanded my network by meeting people from various backgrounds. These exchanges and discoveries strengthened my open-mindedness and adaptability.

And for the future?

I still have two years of studies left, and I am keeping the possibility of going abroad to discover new countries and cultures. If I take this opportunity, I would consider going, perhaps to China, Japan, or North America.
